9

Methodology

A transparent sequence from service-flow simulation to final recommendation.

1
Simulate current service flow

Virtual visitors move through the configured service stages to calculate waiting, queues and utilization.

2
Generate alternative allocations

Possible counter allocations are created without increasing total available resources.

3
Rank configurations using ML

The trained wait-time model estimates likely congestion and ranks staffing configurations.

4
Verify shortlisted options

Leading ML-ranked configurations are re-tested using detailed simulation.

5
Select final recommendation

Waiting, bottleneck severity, utilization and fairness are evaluated together.
